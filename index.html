Principles

These are not slogans. They are the design constraints that keep a young institution from looking young.

Typography as governance
  • Hierarchy must be obvious, even under stress scanning.

Minimal surface, maximal depth
  • No decorative clutter; every element earns its footprint.
  • Fast, static delivery; complexity belongs in content.
  • Design supports credibility—never competes with it.
International posture
  • Institutional tone: measured, not loud.
